FAQ: Startpage
Ad-Free & Accounts

How does Startpage Ad-Free work?

Startpage Ad-Free is a paid subscription that removes ads and sponsored results from your private search, so you get straight to the answers you came for. Here’s how it works:

  • Choose a monthly or yearly plan and complete checkout
  • Create a private Startpage account. Your account is what activates ad-free search
  • Cancel or re-start your subscription at any time
Are Startpage accounts private?

Yes. Creating a Startpage account doesn’t change how we protect you:

  • Your searches are never linked to your account
  • We never track, store, or sell your search history
  • Your account holds only what’s needed to run your subscription: your email address and billing status
Where is Ad-Free available?

Startpage Ad-Free isn’t available everywhere just yet. We’re starting in select regions and hope to expand soon.

We do not recommend using VPNs from countries other than your own while trying to purchase a subscription or create an account. You may experience errors and be unable to check out, as the experience is being rolled out in phases in certain countries.

Can I activate my Startpage Ad-Free Subscription on the Startpage Mobile App on Android/iOS?

We are rolling out mobile activation in phases. More information coming soon.

I made an account, but I can’t sign up for an Ad-free subscription. Why?

Startpage Ad-Free isn’t available everywhere just yet. If you made an account without buying a subscription, you are not being charged for anything, and we’ll alert you when ad-free is available in your area.

What third parties do you work with for Startpage accounts?

Two third parties help run Ad-Free:

  • Stripe handles payments securely. We never see or store your full payment details
  • WorkOS manages account sign-in. It verifies your login, nothing more

Neither has access to your Startpage searches.

For full details, see our privacy policy.

I deleted my account, but I’m still able to log-in using the email of my old account. What’s going on?

WorkOS (Our partner for log-in + sign-up) treats logging in and signing up as the same action. If you try to log-in on Startpage.com using this email address + the “email sign in code” option, it will automatically sign you up for a brand new account under this email. You are not accessing your recently deleted account.

I cancelled my subscription on Stripe but I still cannot delete my Startpage account. Why?

If you cancel your subscription on Stripe, it may still be active through the billing period. While it is still active, you cannot delete your Startpage account from your portal. You will be able to delete your account once the subscription is no longer active. If you would like to delete your account sooner, please reach out to us.

I am getting billing and account emails on different addresses. I can’t sign in to my Stripe billing portal with my Startpage account email.

Your Startpage account and your Stripe billing account do not have to be set up with the same email address. Your Startpage email will be visible on your Account Settings page, and your Stripe email will be visible in your Billing portal, also accessible from your Account Settings page.

Can I change either of these emails? We currently do not support changing email addresses through the Startpage or Stripe portals. Please reach out to us with any concerns.

Can students get a free Ad-free subscription?

Yes. Students with a .edu email address get one year free of Ad-free Startpage. We do not use any third parties to verify student status, we only check if you have a valid .edu email. We currently cannot offer ad-free subscriptions to students without a .edu email. If you believe you should have gotten a free subscription and did not, email subscribers@startpage.com for help.

Can I use my StartMail account to sign in to Startpage?

Startpage and StartMail are separate products, and our ad-free and their personal and business subscriptions are unrelated. You are welcome to use your StartMail email to sign up for an account on Startpage.

Questions? Email us at: subscribers@startpage.com
